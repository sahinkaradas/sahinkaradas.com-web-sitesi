When you click "Accept" in the cookie notice or turn on Marketing in Settings, you allow:

1. the Meta Pixel to run on sahinkaradas.com and Meta cookies (\_fbp, \_fbc) to be placed in your browser,
2. your IP address, browser and device information, the pages you view and your clicks on the WhatsApp and booking buttons to be processed to measure ad results and show you ads on Instagram / Facebook,
3. this information to be transferred to Meta and to Meta's servers abroad, mainly in the USA (KVKK Art. 9/6-a).

**Possible risks of the transfer abroad:** Your information may be processed in a country that does not offer the level of protection you have in Türkiye. That country's public authorities may access it under their own laws. Meta may also process it under its own privacy policy. Using your rights against Meta may not be as easy as in Türkiye.

You don't have to consent. If you don't, every part of the site works the same, and you can still message me on WhatsApp and book a call.

You can withdraw your consent any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of the page. When you do, the pixel no longer loads and the Meta cookies on this site are deleted. Withdrawal does not affect anything done before it.
