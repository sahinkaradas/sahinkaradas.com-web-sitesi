This notice explains how I process your personal data under Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK"). It applies to anyone who contacts me via WhatsApp, Instagram, email or phone, books a call on Calendly, uses my services or visits sahinkaradas.com.

## Purposes and legal grounds

| Purpose | Legal ground (KVKK Art. 5) |
| --- | --- |
| Replying to your messages and enquiries | Directly related to entering into a contract (Art. 5/2-c) |
| Scheduling the intro call and reminding you of it (Calendly) | Directly related to entering into a contract (Art. 5/2-c) |
| Providing the service: setting up, managing and reporting Meta ads and Instagram / WhatsApp automation | Performance of a contract (Art. 5/2-c) |
| Keeping records so I don't have to ask for your information again (Google Drive and Sheets) | Performance of a contract (Art. 5/2-c) |
| Issuing invoices and meeting tax obligations | Legal obligation (Art. 5/2-ç) |
| Protecting my rights in a possible dispute | Establishing, exercising or protecting a right (Art. 5/2-e) |

## Transfers abroad

The servers of the apps above are outside Türkiye, mainly in the USA and EU countries. When I use these apps, your data is transferred abroad. I carry out these transfers in line with the conditions in KVKK Art. 9. Transfers abroad through the Meta Pixel happen only with your [explicit consent](@@PAGE:en/consent/@@).

When you contact me via WhatsApp, Instagram or Calendly, you use these platforms with your own account, so their own privacy policies also apply.

## How long I keep data

| Data | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Messages and call records where you did not buy a service | 2 years from the last contact |
| Customer records (service details, messages) | 10 years after the service relationship ends (general limitation period, Turkish Code of Obligations) |
| Invoices and accounting records | 5 years under the Tax Procedure Law |
| Website technical logs | The hosting provider's own period; I don't keep these logs separately |
| Meta Pixel cookies | Up to 90 days |
| Your cookie choice | 6 months; then I ask again |

When the period ends, I delete, destroy or anonymise the data.

## Your rights (KVKK Art. 11)

You can ask me to:

- tell you whether your personal data is processed,
- give you information about it if it is,
- tell you why it is processed and whether it is used for that purpose,
- tell you who it is transferred to in Türkiye or abroad,
- correct it if it is incomplete or wrong,
- delete or destroy it under the conditions in KVKK Art. 7,
- notify the recipients of any correction, deletion or destruction,
- object to a result against you that comes solely from automated analysis,
- compensate you if you suffer damage due to unlawful processing.

## How to apply

Write to [destek@sahinkaradas.com](mailto:destek@sahinkaradas.com). Writing from the email address you gave me before helps me verify your identity. You can also apply with a secure electronic signature or mobile signature. For a written (postal) application, you can ask me for my full postal address by email.

Your application should include your name, Turkish ID number (or nationality and passport number if you are not a Turkish citizen), address, email or phone, and your request.

I will reply free of charge within 30 days at the latest. If the action involves an extra cost, I may charge the fee set by the Personal Data Protection Board. If you find my reply insufficient or I don't reply in time, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board within 30 days of learning of my reply, and in any case within 60 days of your application.
