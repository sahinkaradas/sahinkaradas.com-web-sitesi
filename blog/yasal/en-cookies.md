sahinkaradas.com uses only two kinds of cookies: those needed to run the site, and the Meta Pixel, which runs only if you allow it. The site uses no analytics tool, no Google Fonts and no other external resources.

## What is a cookie

A cookie is a small text file saved in your browser when you visit a site. This site also uses "local storage" (localStorage), which does the same job. In this policy I call both "cookies".

## Cookies I use

| Name | Type | What it does | Set by | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| sk-lang | Necessary (preference) | Remembers your language (Turkish / English) | sahinkaradas.com | Until you delete it |
| sk-theme | Necessary (preference) | Remembers your light / dark theme choice | sahinkaradas.com | Until you delete it |
| sk-consent | Necessary | Remembers your cookie choice | sahinkaradas.com | 6 months |
| \_fbp | Marketing (consent only) | Lets Meta recognise your browser across visits; ad measurement and targeting | Meta Pixel | 90 days |
| \_fbc | Marketing (consent only) | Records which ad you came from if you arrived from an Instagram / Facebook ad | Meta Pixel | 90 days |

When the Meta Pixel runs, Meta may also use cookies on its own domain (facebook.com). These are covered by Meta's own cookie policy.

## Necessary cookies

sk-lang, sk-theme and sk-consent stay on your device only. They are not sent to me or anyone else. They don't need consent because they run the site and remember a choice you made.

## Meta Pixel (marketing cookies)

The Meta Pixel runs only when you click "Accept" in the cookie notice or turn on Marketing in Settings. Nothing is sent to Meta before you consent.

- **What goes to Meta:** your IP address, browser and device information, the pages you view, and your clicks on the WhatsApp and booking buttons. Meta may match this with your Meta account.
- **Why:** to measure how many people my ads bring to the site and how many of them message me, and to show ads on Instagram and Facebook to people who visited the site.
- **Where it is processed:** on Meta's servers abroad. Details are in the [Explicit Consent Text](@@PAGE:en/consent/@@).

## How to change your choice

Click "Cookie settings" at the very bottom of the page to reopen the settings panel. When you withdraw consent, the pixel no longer loads and the \_fbp and \_fbc cookies on this site are deleted. You can also delete or block Meta's own cookies, and all cookies, in your browser settings.

## Moving to other sites

When you click a WhatsApp, Instagram or Calendly link, you move to that site, and its own cookie policy applies there.
